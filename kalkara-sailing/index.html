What you can learn

Courses on the water

Start from zero or sharpen what you already know. Every course runs on the boat, in the harbour and outside it, and the programme is agreed with you before the first session.

Learn to sail

Your first days on the water: handling the boat, setting the sails and moving safely around the deck, with an instructor beside you.

Price on request

Sail training

With a trainer on board: team building, sail trimming and the spinnaker and gennaker course. Half a day up to two weeks, solo, double handed or full crew.

Half day from 400 €

ISSA practice with an instructor

Five programmes from Yacht Crew to Boat Master, each with a set number of days on the water and an instructor on board.

From 1 300 € per person

Regatta and racing training

Training for the start line: tactics, racing rules, manoeuvres and crew roles, with sessions run in racing mode.

Price on request

Private lessons

One to one, or with your own group, at your pace and on the dates that suit you.

Price on request

How it works

Three steps from here to the water

  1. 01

    Choose your course and date

    Pick a course and a date that suits you and send the form. If you are not sure yet, write to us and we will suggest the right course.

  2. 02

    We confirm on WhatsApp

    We answer on WhatsApp, agree the time and place with you, and tell you what to bring.

  3. 03

    Meet at Marina Kalkara

    We meet you at the marina in Kalkara, Malta. On the boat, the instructor goes through the plan for the day.
